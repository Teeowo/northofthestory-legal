North of the Story Publisher

A private posting tool for the North of the Story channel.

North of the Story makes short videos about Canadian life, news and culture. The Publisher is the tool its creator uses to post those videos to the channel's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

How it works

  1. The creator finishes a video and writes its caption.
  2. The creator reviews and approves each video before anything is posted.
  3. The Publisher sends the approved video to the channel's own account through each platform's official API. On TikTok, the video goes to the creator's TikTok inbox, and the creator finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.

Who can use it

The Publisher runs on the creator's own computer and is used only for accounts the creator owns and has connected. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect anything from viewers.
