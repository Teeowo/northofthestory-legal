Privacy Policy

North of the Story Publisher · Last updated 7 November 2026

Summary

The Publisher posts North of the Story's own videos to North of the Story's own accounts. It collects nothing from viewers or other TikTok users, and it does not sell, share or analyse anyone's data.

What it stores

  • Login tokens for each connected account (for TikTok: the access and refresh tokens and the account's open ID). They are encrypted and stored only on the creator's own computer.
  • The account's display name, to show which account a video will go to.
  • The videos and captions North of the Story is posting, and a record of each post (when it was sent and the platform's reference for it).

What it does with it

Only uploads videos to the connected account and checks whether each upload succeeded. Nothing is sent to any third party other than the platform the video is being posted to.

What it does not do

  • It does not read followers, messages, comments, likes or any other user's information.
  • It has no ads, analytics or tracking, and this website sets no cookies.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Revoking the app's access in the platform's settings (in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) makes the stored token useless immediately; the creator then deletes it from the Publisher. Copies of videos are deleted 30 days after they are posted.

Children

The Publisher is not directed at children and is used only by the North of the Story creator.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: send a message to North of the Story on its Facebook Page or TikTok account.
